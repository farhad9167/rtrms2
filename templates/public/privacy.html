Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

From Restaurant Owners: Google account info (name, email) used to sign in; restaurant details you provide (name, address, country, menu, branding images); subscription payment verification details (sender name, account/phone number, transaction notes) — we do not collect or store card numbers or full bank credentials.

From Restaurant Customers: Orders placed through a restaurant's menu; approximate device location, only if the restaurant has enabled location-based order verification. We do not require customer accounts.

2. How We Use Information

To operate account creation, ordering, kitchen-printing, and billing features; to verify and activate subscriptions; to back up restaurant data; to display owner-submitted platform reviews; to improve and maintain the Service.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers who host our servers, or if required by law.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Restaurant operational data is stored on the restaurant's local server and backed up to our central server. Owners may delete their own sales data (by day, month, or year) at any time from their dashboard.

5. Your Choices

Owners can update or delete menu, branding, and sales data from their dashboard at any time. Owners can request account deletion via our Contact page. Customers can decline location permission; declining may limit ordering from outside the restaurant's local network, per that restaurant's settings.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, including access controls on our admin panels and encrypted connections. No system is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for restaurant business use and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: see our Contact page.
